Privacy Policy — Mahjong

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Mahjong app (iOS and Android), published by LFH Inc. (LoudFH). The in-app About → Privacy Policy screen describes these practices.

Who we are

Mahjong (“the app”) is a hobby companion for mahjong and mah-jongg players. It offers a news feed, discovery of third-party instructional videos, a Store tab that is currently Coming Soon, and friend play for a signed-in player. You sign in with an email address and a password. The app sends a confirmation email so you can confirm that address. Favorites stay on the device until the first sign-in. After you sign in, the articles and videos you mark as favorites stay with the account.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the app treats information, including the player account, the confirmation email, favorites, and friend play.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or in-app purchases.

What we store, and why

The app stores the following for a player account:

  • Display name. The name you choose, so the app can show it with your account and at a friend table.
  • Email address. This is the address you use to sign in. The app sends a confirmation email to that address.
  • Password. You choose a password so you can sign in with that email. We keep it only so the account can be opened with that email and password. We do not use it for analytics or advertising.
  • Favorites. The articles and videos you mark as favorites. They stay on the device until the first sign-in. After you sign in, they stay with the account and come back when you sign in again.
  • Friend play. When you play with friends, the app uses your signed-in account, the display name at the table, and the invite for that table, so the people at the table can play together.

We store the display name, email, password, and favorites so you can sign in and so saved articles and videos can be restored with that account. We do not use them for analytics or advertising.

The app may also keep ordinary on-device interface state (for example, navigation). That state stays on your device. Favorites you mark before the first sign-in stay on the device with that state.

Email, password, and the confirmation email

You sign in with the email address and password you choose. The app sends a confirmation email to that address. Open the confirmation email and confirm the address, then return to the app and sign in. The confirmation page for that email is confirmed.html.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect contacts, photos, precise GPS, health data, payment card numbers, or government IDs. It does not run its own analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting. Push notifications are not enabled in this version. We do not collect payment details or precise location through the app’s own screens.

Third-party video content

Videos are not owned or created by this app. They remain the property of their respective creators and owners. This app only links to or embeds third-party videos for educational and entertainment discovery. Thumbnails and embeds are requested from YouTube (Google). The app does not claim ownership of those videos.

Outbound links and other companies’ policies

If you open a video on YouTube, follow a news image or page on another site, or (in a future update) continue to Amazon, you leave this app. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control what they collect.

  • YouTube / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/privacy

News feed and images

News stories in this version are sample articles bundled with the app. Images may load from public hosts (for example Wikimedia Commons). Loading an image is a network request to that host; we do not use those requests to build an advertising profile.

Future Amazon store

The Store tab is Coming Soon. When shopping is enabled, purchases will be completed on Amazon, not in this app. Amazon’s privacy policy will apply to checkout. Any affiliate disclosure will be shown in the shop at that time. We still will not sell your personal data.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience interested in mahjong. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app needs network access to sign in, deliver the confirmation email flow, save favorites after the first sign-in, play with friends, and to load news images, video thumbnails, and YouTube playback. It does not require camera, microphone, contacts, or tracking permission. On iOS, we do not use App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

Data retention

Before the first sign-in, favorites stay on the device. Uninstalling the app removes favorites and other data stored only on that device. After you sign in, the display name, email, and favorites are kept with the account so signing in again can restore them. Friend-play invites are kept while that table is open. Questions about an account: rob@loudfh.com.

Your choices

You choose the display name, the email address, and the password you register. You confirm the email from the confirmation email. Favorites stay on the device until that first sign-in. You can stop using third-party embeds by not opening Videos, and you can avoid outbound sites by not tapping YouTube (or, later, Amazon) links.

International users

The app may be used from more than one country. The display name, email, password, and favorites are stored so you can sign in and restore them. Friend play uses the same account. Network requests to YouTube, image hosts, and (later) Amazon may be processed in the United States or other regions those providers use.

Changes

If the app starts collecting additional information, or uses information in a new way, we will update this policy and the in-app Privacy screen before that behavior ships.
