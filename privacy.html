Privacy Policy — Mahjong

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This document is the hosted-ready privacy policy for the Mahjong app (iOS and Android). The in-app About → Privacy Policy screen shows the same policy.

  • Contact for privacy questions: **rob@loudfh.com**.

Who we are

Mahjong (“the app”) is a hobby companion for mahjong and mah-jongg players. It offers a news feed, discovery of third-party instructional videos, and a Store tab that is currently Coming Soon. The app does not offer account registration or sign-in in this version.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the app treats information in the current MVP, for Apple App Store and Google Play review.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. This MVP does not include a login, profile, or in-app purchases.

What we collect in this MVP

The app does not implement its own analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or account systems. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, payment details, or precise location through the app’s own screens.

The app stores only ordinary on-device UI state (for example, navigation). That state stays on your device and is not uploaded to a Mahjong backend — this MVP has no app server of its own.

What we do not collect

This MVP does not collect: account credentials; contacts; photos from your library (except what you choose if a future feature asks); precise GPS; health data; payment card numbers; or government IDs. Push notifications are not enabled in this version.

Third-party video content

Videos are not owned or created by this app. They remain the property of their respective creators and owners. This app only links to or embeds third-party videos for educational and entertainment discovery. Thumbnails and embeds are requested from YouTube (Google). The app does not claim ownership of those videos.

Outbound links and other companies’ policies

If you open a video on YouTube, follow a news image or page on another site, or (in a future update) continue to Amazon, you leave this app. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control what they collect.

  • YouTube / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/privacy

News feed and images

News stories in this MVP are sample articles bundled with the app. Images may load from public hosts (for example Wikimedia Commons). Loading an image is a network request to that host; we do not use those requests to build an advertising profile.

Future Amazon store

The Store tab is Coming Soon. When shopping is enabled, purchases will be completed on Amazon, not in this app. Amazon’s privacy policy will apply to checkout. Any affiliate disclosure will be shown in the shop at that time. We still will not sell your personal data.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience interested in mahjong. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app needs network access to load news images, video thumbnails, and YouTube playback. It does not require camera, microphone, contacts, or tracking permission in this MVP. On iOS, we do not use App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

Data retention

Because this MVP does not operate a user database, we do not retain personal profiles on a server. You can remove local app data by uninstalling the app.

Your choices

You can stop using third-party embeds by not opening Videos, and you can avoid outbound sites by not tapping YouTube (or, later, Amazon) links. Uninstalling the app deletes local data stored by the app.

International users

The app may be used from more than one country. Network requests to YouTube, image hosts, and (later) Amazon may be processed in the United States or other regions those providers use.

Changes

If the app starts collecting more data (for example analytics or accounts), we will update this policy and the in-app Privacy screen before that behavior ships.
