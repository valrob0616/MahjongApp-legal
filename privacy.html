Privacy Policy — Mahjong

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This is the privacy policy for the Mahjong app (iOS and Android), published by LFH Inc. (LoudFH). The in-app About → Privacy Policy screen describes these practices.

Who we are

Mahjong (“the app”) is a hobby companion for mahjong and mah-jongg players. It offers a news feed, discovery of third-party instructional videos, and a Store tab that is currently Coming Soon. The app saves a player account: a display name, an email address, and the articles and videos you mark as favorites.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the app treats information, including the player account.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or in-app purchases.

What we store, and why

The app stores the following for a player account:

  • Display name. The name you choose, so the app can show it with your account.
  • Email address. This is your identity. The same email restores your registration and your favorites after you reinstall the app.
  • Favorites. The articles and videos you mark as favorites, so they stay with the account and come back when that email is used again.

We store the display name, email, and favorites so your registration and saved articles and videos can be restored with that email. We do not use them for analytics or advertising.

The app may also keep ordinary on-device interface state (for example, navigation). That state stays on your device.

No password — the email is the identity

There is no password. The email is the only identity for the account. Knowing the email is enough to open that account, including the display name and the article and video favorites saved to it. Anyone who knows the email can open the account. Use an email only if you accept that.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect a password. It does not collect contacts, photos, precise GPS, health data, payment card numbers, or government IDs. It does not run its own analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting. Push notifications are not enabled in this version. We do not collect payment details or precise location through the app’s own screens.

Third-party video content

Videos are not owned or created by this app. They remain the property of their respective creators and owners. This app only links to or embeds third-party videos for educational and entertainment discovery. Thumbnails and embeds are requested from YouTube (Google). The app does not claim ownership of those videos.

Outbound links and other companies’ policies

If you open a video on YouTube, follow a news image or page on another site, or (in a future update) continue to Amazon, you leave this app. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control what they collect.

  • YouTube / Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/privacy

News feed and images

News stories in this version are sample articles bundled with the app. Images may load from public hosts (for example Wikimedia Commons). Loading an image is a network request to that host; we do not use those requests to build an advertising profile.

Future Amazon store

The Store tab is Coming Soon. When shopping is enabled, purchases will be completed on Amazon, not in this app. Amazon’s privacy policy will apply to checkout. Any affiliate disclosure will be shown in the shop at that time. We still will not sell your personal data.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience interested in mahjong. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app needs network access to save and restore a player account (display name, email, and favorites), and to load news images, video thumbnails, and YouTube playback. It does not require camera, microphone, contacts, or tracking permission. On iOS, we do not use App Tracking Transparency because we do not track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

Data retention

The display name, email, and favorites are kept so the same email can restore the registration and favorites after you reinstall. Uninstalling the app removes data stored only on that device. The saved account remains. Entering the same email again opens that account, including the favorites. Questions about an account: rob@loudfh.com.

Your choices

You choose the display name and the email you register. Because there is no password, anyone who knows the email can open the account and the favorites saved to it. You can stop using third-party embeds by not opening Videos, and you can avoid outbound sites by not tapping YouTube (or, later, Amazon) links.

International users

The app may be used from more than one country. The display name, email, and favorites are stored so they can be restored with that email. Network requests to YouTube, image hosts, and (later) Amazon may be processed in the United States or other regions those providers use.

Changes

If the app starts collecting additional information, or uses information in a new way, we will update this policy and the in-app Privacy screen before that behavior ships.
